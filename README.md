Welcome to Quit Quest—a passion project dedicated to helping people quit smoking and improve their lives. Quit Quest is a stop-smoking platform designed to support users on their journey to becoming smoke-free.

Goals

Quit Quest aims to:

Provide a user-friendly platform to track progress since quitting.

Offer motivational tools, such as visual savings trackers and milestone achievements.

Encourage users to stay committed by showing tangible benefits of quitting, like money saved and health improvements.

Vision for the Future

This project is more than just a simple web app. We envision Quit Quest evolving into a full-fledged mobile app that:

Includes expanded health tracking features, such as lung health improvements and exercise recommendations.

Offers personalized tips and strategies for staying smoke-free.

Integrates with wearable devices to provide real-time feedback and motivation.
